Onboarding Documentation Automation

80%+ Less Preparation Time

Identified repetitive data entry across a 30+ document onboarding package and redesigned the process around one employee source record — reducing preparation from roughly 30–45 minutes to less than five minutes per employee.

01
The challenge

The same information was being entered again and again.

While working through the onboarding process, I noticed HR repeatedly entering the same employee information across more than 30 documents. Names, dates, departments, job information, and other recurring data had to be re-keyed throughout the packet.

Preparing documentation for a single employee could take roughly 30–45 minutes. In a high-volume hiring environment, that repetitive work consumed significant HR time and created unnecessary opportunities for inconsistent or mistyped information.

02
My role

I saw an opportunity to enter the data once.

  • Mapped recurring employee information across the entire onboarding documentation package.
  • Designed a central employee record to serve as the single source for repeated information.
  • Connected 30+ documents to that source so common fields populated automatically.
  • Built and refined the process for practical HR use, reducing manual preparation while maintaining standardized documents.
03
The solution

Enter once. Reuse everywhere.

Instead of treating each onboarding document as a separate task, I redesigned the package as one connected process. HR entered employee information into a central source record, and recurring fields populated throughout the required documents.

The result retained the organization's existing forms while removing much of the repetitive administrative work required to prepare them.

Process redesign

One source record. 30+ connected documents.

The automation changed how the documentation package was prepared without requiring HR to abandon the forms the organization already used.

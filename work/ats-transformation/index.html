Recruiting Technology Transformation

From Manual Recruiting to an Integrated ATS

Helped evaluate, select, configure, test, and operationalize iCIMS while standardizing recruiting workflows and building internal system capability.

Recruiting WorkflowiCIMS
ApplicantsScreenInterviewOfferHire
01
The challenge

What needed to change.

The organization needed a scalable way to manage recruiting activity, candidate data, hiring workflows, and reporting. Manual and disconnected processes made it harder to maintain consistency, monitor candidate status, and create a reliable data foundation for recruiting decisions.

02
My role

What I did.

  • Participated in needs assessment and platform evaluation.
  • Supported the selection of iCIMS based on recruiting and business requirements.
  • Translated recruiting needs into standardized system workflows.
  • Supported configuration, testing, process documentation, and adoption.
  • Troubleshot issues and became a key internal resource after implementation.
  • Helped ensure internal capability remained after implementation rather than relying solely on external support.
03
The solution

Built for practical use.

The implementation centralized recruiting activity inside an ATS and gave the organization a more consistent way to manage candidates, workflows, status visibility, and data. The work combined technology implementation with process standardization and operational ownership.

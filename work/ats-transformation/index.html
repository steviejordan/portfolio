Recruiting Technology Transformation

~4 Weeks → ≤1 Week Time-to-Fill

Recognized that a high-volume recruiting problem was really a process and technology problem, then helped take the solution from concept to implementation by introducing iCIMS, redesigning recruiting workflows, and managing the system after launch.

01
The challenge

Recruiting volume had outgrown the process.

Recruiting relied heavily on manual application tracking, spreadsheets, paper resumes, and disconnected candidate communication. In a high-volume environment, recruiters spent significant time locating applications, updating status, and keeping the process organized.

Some high-interest positions could generate hundreds of applicants in a short period. I recognized that the problem was not simply recruiter workload — the organization needed a scalable recruiting system and a better process around it.

02
My role

From opportunity to operating system.

  • Identified the opportunity to replace fragmented recruiting activity with a centralized applicant tracking system.
  • Defined recruiting requirements, evaluated solutions, and helped select iCIMS as the future-state platform.
  • Translated the desired process into configured workflows, testing, documentation, and recruiter adoption.
  • Managed the system after implementation, resolving issues, maintaining workflows, and serving as an internal iCIMS resource.
03
The solution

One recruiting workflow instead of disconnected steps.

iCIMS became the central source for applicant records, resume search, candidate status, recruiting workflow, and communication. Recruiters could search and filter candidate information digitally instead of working through paper files and separate tracking spreadsheets.

The implementation also introduced more consistent recruiting stages and text-enabled candidate communication, while giving the team a system that could continue to be managed and improved internally after launch.

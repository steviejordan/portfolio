RECRUITMENT TECHNOLOGY

iCIMS ATS Implementation

Identified an opportunity to improve recruiting operations, helped take the solution from concept through implementation, and managed the system after launch.

The Challenge

Recruiting processes lacked the structure and visibility needed to efficiently manage candidates, requisitions, and hiring activity. I saw an opportunity to improve the process through a centralized applicant tracking system.

Rather than simply working within the existing process, I helped develop the solution from the initial idea into a live recruiting platform.

From Concept to Launch

  • Identified recruiting needs and helped define ATS requirements.
  • Supported workflow design, configuration, testing, and implementation.
  • Coordinated stakeholders and supported user readiness for launch.
  • Managed iCIMS after go-live and continued improving the system.

Business Impact

  • Created a centralized recruiting and candidate-management process.
  • Improved visibility into requisition and candidate activity.
  • Standardized recruiting workflows across the hiring process.
  • Established a stronger foundation for recruiting data and reporting.

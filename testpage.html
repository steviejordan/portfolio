Enterprise Compensation Planning

100% Workforce Coverage · ~1,200 Employees

Built the organization's first structured annual compensation-planning tool, giving executive leaders decision-ready pay, performance, history, and budget information while restricting each planning view to the workforce data relevant to that leader.

01
The challenge

What needed to change.

The organization was establishing its first formal annual compensation-planning process. Leaders needed more than a roster of salaries — they needed enough context to make informed decisions, understand the financial impact of proposed increases, and quickly focus on the employees within their area of responsibility.

The process also needed controls so executives could work with the information they needed without receiving broader compensation data outside their planning scope.

02
My role

What I did.

  • Expanded an initial data-organizing request into an enterprise compensation decision-support tool.
  • Combined current pay, compensation history, performance, tenure, promotion history, and other decision factors into one review experience.
  • Built interactive filtering, proposed-increase fields, automated salary calculations, and real-time budget controls.
  • Created leader-specific planning workbooks and protected key fields to maintain appropriate access and data integrity.
03
The solution

Decision-ready information in one place.

Rather than asking leaders to research employee information across multiple reports and systems, I brought the relevant decision data into one structured planning experience.

Executives could filter and segment their workforce, review current and historical compensation context, enter proposed increases in designated fields, and immediately see the effect on individual salaries and the available compensation budget.

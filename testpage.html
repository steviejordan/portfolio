Corrective Coaching & Leader Capability

120+ Leaders Trained

Replaced a complicated corrective-action approach with a clearer policy, practical coaching framework, and structured leader tool designed to make performance conversations easier to understand, apply, and document consistently.

01
The challenge

What needed to change.

The existing corrective-action process was complex and difficult for leaders to apply consistently. Managers needed clearer guidance on when and how to address performance or conduct concerns, but they also needed a practical structure for the conversation itself.

A policy alone would not solve the problem. Leaders needed an approach they could understand, explain to employees, and confidently use in real situations.

02
My role

What I did.

  • Redesigned the corrective-coaching policy around a simpler, more practical approach to accountability and improvement.
  • Developed a repeatable coaching framework using Situation–Behavior–Impact principles and other manager-coaching best practices.
  • Built a leader conversation and documentation tool capturing observable behavior, impact, employee perspective, commitments, and future expectations.
  • Personally rolled out the approach to more than 120 frontline and senior leaders.
03
The solution

Policy translated into practice.

The final approach connected three pieces that leaders could actually use together: a simplified policy, a repeatable coaching method, and a structured tool for guiding and documenting the conversation.

Instead of focusing only on disciplinary action, the framework directed leaders toward observable behavior, business or team impact, the employee's perspective, clear expectations, and specific commitments for improvement.
